Frequently asked questions

Is this video downloader free?

Yes. It is completely free, with no sign-up, no app to install and no watermark on your downloads.

How do I download an Instagram reel?

In Instagram tap Share → Copy link on the reel, paste the link here, press Fetch, choose a quality and press Download.

Can I download only the audio as MP3?

Yes. After fetching a link, open the Audio only (MP3) tab and choose 128, 192 or 320 kbps.

Which video qualities are available?

Anything from 360p up to the highest quality the original post has, up to 4K. Many reels are only published in 720p or 1080p.

Why can't I download some videos?

Private posts, stories from private accounts and videos that need a login cannot be downloaded. Make sure the post is public.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. It works in any modern browser on Android, iPhone, Windows and Mac.
